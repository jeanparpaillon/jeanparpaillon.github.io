## Context

FP7-ICT was the European Union's research and innovation funding programme for
information and communication technologies. Its calls attract far more
proposals than can be funded, and the selection relies on independent experts
from the field.

## What I did

Appointed as an independent expert, and evaluated project submissions for
FP7-ICT: assessing technical soundness, innovation potential, impact and the
credibility of the consortium behind each proposal.

## Outcome

A view of European R&D from the funding side -- how programmes are built, how
proposals are judged, and what separates the ones that get funded. It has
informed every collaborative research project I have taken part in since.
