## Context

A banking IT services organization wanted to strengthen the DevOps skills of its
development engineers and provide a practical understanding of modern software
delivery practices.

The training was designed specifically for developers, connecting DevOps
principles with their day-to-day development workflow.

## What I did

I designed and delivered a hands-on training program covering the complete development and delivery workflow:

- *DevOps foundations* — principles, culture and software lifecycle
- *Git & GitHub* — source control and collaborative development
- *Containerization* & Docker — building and running containerized applications
- *CI/CD & GitHub Actions* — automated build, test and delivery pipelines
- *Artifact management & Artifactory* — managing and distributing build artifacts
- *DevSecOps foundations* — integrating security practices into the development lifecycle

The course combined concepts with practical exercises, focusing on tools and workflows developers can directly apply to their projects.

## Outcome

Development engineers gained a practical, end-to-end understanding of the DevOps
software delivery lifecycle, from source code management through automated
pipelines and artifact management to containerized delivery.

The training also introduced security as an integral part of the delivery
pipeline, providing the foundations for adopting DevSecOps practices in a
banking IT environment.

After 6 months, DevOps skills have been integrated and IT services have
implemented the mentioned tools.
