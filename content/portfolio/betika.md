## Context

Betika operates a sport betting platform with more than 5 million users. The
existing system had reached its limits, and the business needed a platform that
could take the load, evolve quickly, and be operated by the in-house team.

## What I did

Architect and lead developer of the rewrite:

- Rewrote the platform from scratch, 100% Elixir, on a gRPC micro-services
  architecture.
- Led the development team on the betting core engine -- the highest-throughput
  and most correctness-critical part of the system.
- Planned and carried out the migration of the production system and its MySQL
  data set, without service interruption for existing users.
- Set up the CI/CD processes for the new platform.
- Mentored junior developers on Elixir and on distributed system design.

## Outcome

A production platform rebuilt on a single, coherent stack, with the team able to
own and extend it.
