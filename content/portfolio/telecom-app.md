## Context

A long-term collaboration with a telecom technology company developing software for carrier-grade network appliances.

Over several years, I supported the evolution of the product and its technical architecture, working closely with engineering and management teams.

The engagement progressively extended beyond individual development projects into a trusted senior consulting role, providing architectural continuity and technical guidance as the product and its requirements evolved.

## What I did

I acted as **software architect, technical lead and senior technical consultant**, with responsibilities spanning both strategic and hands-on technical work.

My contributions included:

* Defining and evolving the software architecture of distributed telecom appliances
* Providing technical leadership and guidance to engineering teams
* Supporting strategic technology choices and long-term technical direction
* Designing and reviewing distributed system components and interfaces
* Advising on product evolution, maintainability and technical risks
* Contributing hands-on expertise when complex technical issues required deeper investigation
* Bridging product requirements, telecom constraints and software architecture

The technical environment included **Erlang and Elixir** for distributed and fault-tolerant software, **SNMP** for network management and integration, and Linux-based telecom appliances.

## Outcome

The long-term nature of the collaboration provided **architectural continuity and senior technical leadership** across multiple stages of the product lifecycle.

Rather than focusing only on individual implementations, my role was to help maintain a coherent technical direction over time: supporting engineering decisions, anticipating architectural consequences, reducing technical risk and helping the organization evolve a complex telecom product sustainably.
