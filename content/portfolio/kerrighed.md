## Context

Kerrighed is a Single-System Image operating system: a cluster of machines made
to look and behave like a single large SMP machine, so that applications run on
it unmodified. Born as research at Inria, it was industrialized by the Kerlabs
spin-off -- and I followed it across both.

## What I did

**Inria, HPC Engineer (2005 -- 2007)**

- Kerrighed OS integration on Debian, CentOS and the OSCAR HPC suite of tools.
- Release manager of the OSCAR HPC suite of tools.
- Testbed cluster platform setup and operations.

**Kerlabs, architect and developer of HPC systems (2007 -- 2010)**

- Setup and operations of a 256-node experimental Kerrighed cluster.
- Designed and implemented a cluster management suite: resource allocation,
  network management, A/C monitoring and control (SNMP, Python, web).
- OS integration across Cray CX1, liquid-cooled super workstations and standard
  clusters, on Debian, XtreemOS, CentOS and Mandriva.
- HPC solution architecture for customers.

## Outcome

A research operating system taken all the way to customer hardware, plus the
publications that came with it -- *Clustering the Kernel* and *OSCAR
KernelPicker: Handling Clients Kernels*.
