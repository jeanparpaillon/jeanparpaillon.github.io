## Context

*Poussin sur la Banquise* is an adventure-based art project: a 6.50m scow -- a
small, flat-bottomed racing hull -- prepared for Arctic navigation, and the
voyage turned into a show. It ran under L'Insolite Compagnie, of which I was
president from 2017 to 2024.

## What I did

- Producer of the project: funding, partnerships, planning and logistics.
- Preparation of the boat for high-latitude sailing.
- Turned the navigation into an art show, with the company's artists.

## Outcome

An expedition and a show, delivered by a small volunteer team over seven years.

Not software -- but the same job as any other project here: an ambitious
objective, hard constraints, a team to carry it, and no option but to make the
thing actually work.
