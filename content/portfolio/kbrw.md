## Context

KBRW builds a new generation Order Management System for large retailers --
the system that tracks every order across stores, warehouses and carriers.
Retail peaks are unforgiving: the platform has to stay available and consistent
under bursts of traffic, on a data model that differs for every customer.

## What I did

- Back-end development in Erlang and Elixir, plus front-end work on the
  operator interfaces.
- Architecture work on the distributed core and its Riak-based storage.
- Integration, deployment and DevOps: high-availability topology, automated
  provisioning with Chef, and the release pipeline.

## Outcome

A distributed order management platform running in production for retail
customers, deployable and operable as a repeatable, automated process.
