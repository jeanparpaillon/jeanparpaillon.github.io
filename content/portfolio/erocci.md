## Context

**erocci** is a model-driven REST framework developed in Erlang around the **OCCI (Open Cloud Computing Interface)** standard.

The technology was initially developed for **Lizenn** ([see project](/portfolio/lizenn)) to build highly scalable, extensible cloud and IoT services. Its model-driven architecture allows resources, relationships and capabilities to be described through models rather than hard-coded into the framework.

## What I did

I designed and led the development of the framework, combining:

* a **highly scalable Erlang/OTP architecture**
* a **model-driven approach** to resource and API definition
* REST APIs based on the OCCI standard
* an extensible architecture for implementing domain-specific backends

The objective was to provide a generic technology foundation from which different cloud and IoT management services could be built.

## Outcome

erocci evolved from a company technology into an open-source framework and became a **technology foundation for the OCCIware collaborative research project** ([see project](/portfolio/occiware)).

This transition demonstrated the ability of the architecture to move beyond its initial product context and support a larger research and open-source ecosystem around **model-driven cloud and IoT management**.
