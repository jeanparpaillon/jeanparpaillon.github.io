## Context

Mandriva was a Linux distribution editor with an open-source product portfolio
and an R&D activity feeding it -- a company where the distance between a
research result and a shipped product had to be short.

## What I did

**Product Manager (2011 -- 2012)**

- Managed the Pulse2 IT asset management product line.
- Architected the company's R&D projects on cloud computing.

**Chief Innovation Officer (2012)** -- managing the Innovation department:

- Research projects portfolio.
- Industrialization of R&D outcomes.
- Industrial and academic research partnerships.

## Outcome

A product line under clear ownership, and an innovation pipeline connected to
the products it was meant to feed.
