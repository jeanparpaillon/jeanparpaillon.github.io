## Context

Consilience Ventures is a mutualist startup accelerator: a network where
investors, experts and startups exchange capital and services through a
tokenized digital equity model. That model needs a platform its members can
trust -- and a network of experts to feed it.

## What I did

**Head of Architecture (2018 -- 2019)** -- architect and developer of the
accelerator platform:

- Elixir back end with an Ethereum smart-contract layer for the token model.
- GraphQL API with a React front end.
- Set up and operated the Docker Swarm infrastructure, provisioned with Ansible.

**Distributed systems expert (2019 -- 2023)** -- as an expert in the network,
contributed to its development: onboarding other experts, taking part in the
startup screening process, and advising portfolio companies on distributed
systems and technical strategy.

## Outcome

A running platform for the network's members, and five years of involvement in
the community built around it.
