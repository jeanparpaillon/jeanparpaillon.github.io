## Context

Silkan builds HPC and real-time simulation solutions. Cloud computing was
changing how simulation workloads could be delivered, and the company needed
that explored through collaborative R&D rather than in isolation.

## What I did

- Managed R&D partnerships on cloud computing projects, with industrial and
  academic partners.
- Led the cloud computing innovation projects, including cloud rendering for
  simulation workloads.
- Integrated the outcomes of those R&D projects back into internal products.

## Outcome

Collaborative research turned into technology the company's own simulation
solutions could use.
