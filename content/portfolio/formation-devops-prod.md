## Context

A banking IT services organization wanted to modernize its infrastructure
management practices and develop DevOps capabilities within its production
teams.

The objective was to move from traditional infrastructure operations toward
Infrastructure as Code (IaC), version-controlled configuration and automated
deployment practices.

## What I did

I designed and delivered a progressive, hands-on training program for production
engineers over a one-year period.

The program covered the complete infrastructure automation workflow:

- *DevOps and Infrastructure as Code foundations*
- *Git & GitHub* for versioning infrastructure and configuration
- *Docker* for containerized workloads
- *K*ubernetes for container orchestration
- *GitHub Actions* for infrastructure automation and CI/CD
- *Terraform* for declarative infrastructure provisioning
- *Ansible* for configuration management and automation

The approach combined training with progressive adoption of the practices and
tools within the team's operational environment.

## Outcome

After one year, the production teams had integrated Infrastructure as Code and
DevOps practices into their day-to-day work.

The technologies introduced during the program were adopted and implemented
within the IT services organization, turning the training into an effective
skills-transfer and operational transformation initiative rather than a
standalone course.
