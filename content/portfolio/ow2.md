## Context

OW2 is an independent international community committed to making the best and
most reliable enterprise computing infrastructure software available to
everyone -- middleware, application platforms, cloud technologies. Its mission
is as much about the ecosystem around the code as about the code itself.

## What I did

Nine years in OW2's governance, in three successive roles.

**President (2017 -- 2023)** -- building and developing a vibrant open-source
ecosystem: strategy, community, and the consortium's positioning among its
industrial and academic members.

**Board of Directors, individual members' representative (2014 -- 2019)**

- Definition of OW2's position on next-generation R&D programmes (H2020).
- Refinement of the OW2 principles on open-source licensing, and their
  application in the Technology Council.
- Promotion of OW2 towards individual contributors.

**Technical Committee Chairman (2015 -- 2016)**

- Defined and promoted the existence and vision of the Technology Council.
- Increased the synergies between OW2 projects.
- Brought OW2 into the OCCI standardization initiative.
- Drove the enrollment of OW2 in H2020 programmes, and the definition and
  recruitment of an OW2 CTO.

## Outcome

An open-source consortium with a clearer technical governance, a stronger
presence in European R&D programmes, and an ecosystem that outlives any one
mandate.
