## Context

Connected objects were multiplying faster than the software to manage them.
Lizenn was founded in Brittany to build an IoT platform for that gap -- and to
find out whether the idea could carry a company.

## What I did

- Co-founder and technical lead: product definition, architecture and
  development of the IoT platform, on an Erlang stack.
- IoT software engineering: device connectivity, data collection, and the
  service layer above it.
- Pitched and defended the project in innovation programmes and competitions.

## Outcome

- **OW2 Best Project Award**, Innovation category.
- **EIT ICT Labs Idea Challenge** finalist, Internet of Things category.

A first-hand run through the whole path from idea to product to market -- and
the experience that fed everything I have done on innovation since.
